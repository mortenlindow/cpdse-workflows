Pharmaceutical Data Science (PDS) Competence Model. Use the level that best
describes what the group can do TODAY (not what one person could do, and not

  N/A = not relevant to our work
  L1  = Awareness    — we recognise it; we cannot yet do it ourselves
  L2  = Familiarity  — we can do it from a template, with someone to ask
  L3  = Proficiency  — we independently deliver usable work on standard problems
  L4  = Mastery      — we handle non-standard cases; we are who others escalate to
  L5  = Expertise    — we set the standard; we teach, publish, or open-source it
