Three reference models

The CPDSE advantage is consistency. Every engagement, regardless of customer type, is grounded in three reference models.

Pharma

Pharma Value Chain Model

The 15 stages from target identification to market access. We map each customer to the stage(s) they operate in. v0.1.0 covers 6 of 15.

Data Science

PDS Competence Model

Seven domains — Computing & Programming, Data Acquisition & Management, Ethics, Legislation & Privacy, Exploration, Mining & Analysis, ML & AI, Mathematics & Statistics, Visualization & Presentation — over 30 sub-areas. Self-rated L1–L5.

Education

Training from the Back of the Room

Sharon L. Bowman's pedagogical framework — the 4Cs (Connections, Concepts, Concrete Practice, Conclusions) and the Six Trumps brain-based principles. The reference behind our workshop design.

Structural before technical

Most groups expect a list of tools to learn. The higher-leverage moves are usually structural: reproducibility, data management, how knowledge is distributed across the team. Fix one structural gap and every research thread benefits — fix one technical gap and one method gets faster. We name both, and we tell you which is which.

FAQ

Is this an assessment or coaching?

Diagnosis and roadmap. Implementation is on you — or, if useful, on a follow-on engagement (Specific Research Consulting).

Who sees the brief?

Only your group, unless you choose to share it. CPDSE keeps a meta-record of what we learned about our own reference model, never your specifics.

What if our work doesn't fit your reference model?

That's a flag we actively want. We mark it [OUTSIDE MODEL] or [BEYOND REFERENCE] — both feed back into the model and improve the next engagement.

Running an engagement

A human-in-the-loop pipeline. Four prompt steps, two workshops, ~7–8 hours of consultant time per group.

The pipeline at a glance

Passive collection (lab site, grants, pubs, READMEs) (1h)
  → Active intake: questionnaire async + Meeting 1 interview (1h)
  → Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Step 4
  → QA (1h) → Workshop 1 (2h) → Workshop 2 (1.5h) Total consultant time: ~7–8h

How to run each step

Recommended setup: clone the repo locally and run Claude Code from the project root. Steps 1 & 2 can also be run in claude.ai with file attachments — useful when you need a long context window for messy passive material. Save every output as a file in engagements/ENG###-{LastName}/ before moving to the next step.

Inputs: passive vs active

The engagement starts with both. Pull the passive material before Meeting 1 so you can ask sharper questions; treat the active inputs as the source of truth where they conflict with the passive ones.

Passive

  • Group's own internal docs if shared
Active
  • Intake questionnaire (PI fills async)

Design principles

  • Step 1 is factual-only. Gap judgements live in Step 3, never earlier.
  • Structural vs technical is the analytical frame, not a label. It shifts the conversation off "tools to learn."
  • [BEYOND REFERENCE] and [OUTSIDE MODEL] flags are first-class outputs — they improve the model, not error states.
  • "A recommendation that exceeds capacity is a wish, not a recommendation."
  • The brief is handed over at the end of Workshop 1, not before. Workshop 1 produces shared ownership of the diagnosis; the brief is a record, not a script.
